About

Statistics with

I'm Ayunita a Statistics undergraduate who likes the unglamorous half of data science: cleaning, checking, and asking whether the number actually means what everyone thinks it means.

My toolkit spans Python (Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, PyTorch), R and SQL across the whole pipeline preprocessing, exploratory analysis, model development and evaluation, then deployment with Streamlit. I've applied supervised and unsupervised learning and spatial statistics to real classification problems, from mangrove ecosystems to skin disease imagery. Just as often, the real work is translating what a model found into something a non-technical audience can actually act on — that's the part I enjoy most.
